# Galaxy Hotel – Guest Guide

## Welcome
Welcome to the Galaxy Hotel. Our building opened in 1962 as a small guest house for travelling musicians. In 1987 the Moradi family bought it, added three floors and gave it the name it has today. The famous glass dome on the roof was added in 2004 and is now the most photographed part of the hotel. Many of our staff have worked here for more than ten years, and we are proud to welcome guests from more than sixty countries every year. We hope this guide answers your questions. If it does not, our reception team is available 24 hours a day.

## Check-in and check-out
Check-in starts at 15:00. Check-out is by 11:00.
Early check-in from 12:00 costs €20 and depends on availability.
Late check-out until 14:00 costs €30. Staying after 14:00 is charged as a full extra night.

## Breakfast
Breakfast is served in the Orbit restaurant on the 2nd floor.
Weekdays: 07:00–10:30. Weekends: 07:30–11:30.
Breakfast is included for Deluxe rooms. For all other rooms it costs €18 per person. Children under 6 eat for free.

## Swimming pool
The pool is on the 3rd floor. It is open every day from 08:00 to 21:00.
On Fridays the pool closes at 19:00 for deep cleaning.
Swimming caps are required. Children under 12 must be with an adult.

## Gym
The gym is open 24 hours. Use your room key card to enter.

## Wi-Fi
The network name is "Galaxy-Guest". Standard Wi-Fi is free. Premium high-speed Wi-Fi costs €5 per day.

## Parking
Underground parking costs €15 per night. We have 4 electric-car charging spots. Please book them at reception.

## Pets
Dogs up to 15 kg are welcome for €25 per night. Dogs are not allowed in the restaurant or the pool area.
Cats and other animals are not allowed.

## Airport shuttle
The shuttle leaves every hour, on the hour, from 06:00 to 22:00 from the main entrance. The trip to the airport takes about 40 minutes. It costs €12 per person. Please book at least 2 hours before departure.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00. Please keep noise low in the corridors.

## Laundry
Put your clothes in the laundry bag in the wardrobe. If you give it to us before 09:00, we return it the same day by 18:00.

## Key cards
A lost key card costs €10 to replace.

## Smoking
Smoking is not allowed anywhere inside the hotel. We charge a €200 cleaning fee if someone smokes in a room.

## Room types
Standard rooms have one queen bed or two single beds, a desk and a rain shower. Deluxe rooms are larger, have a balcony with a view of the old town, a bathtub and a coffee machine. Family rooms have one king bed and two single beds and can sleep up to four people. All rooms have air conditioning, a safe, a hair dryer and a small fridge.

## Payment
We accept all major credit cards and cash in euros. The full price of the stay is charged at check-in. We also take a €100 deposit per stay for extras. The deposit is returned at check-out if there are no unpaid extras.

## Children
Children of all ages are welcome. Baby cots are free and can be ordered at reception. An extra bed for a child between 6 and 12 years costs €25 per night. Children under 12 must be with an adult in the pool, as described above.

## Accessibility
The hotel has two lifts and step-free access from the main entrance. Four rooms on the 1st floor are adapted for wheelchair users, with wide doors and a roll-in shower. Guide dogs are always welcome and are free of charge.

## Housekeeping
Rooms are cleaned every day between 09:00 and 15:00. If you do not want cleaning, hang the green card on your door. Towels are changed when you leave them on the floor of the bathroom. Bed linen is changed every three days, or on request.

## Room service
Room service is available from 11:00 to 23:00. The menu is in the folder on your desk. A delivery fee of €4 is added to every order.

## Spa
The spa is on the 3rd floor, next to the pool. It offers massages, facial treatments and a sauna. The sauna is open from 10:00 to 20:00. Massages must be booked at least one day in advance at reception. Guests must be 16 or older to use the sauna.

## Meeting rooms
We have two meeting rooms for up to 12 and 30 people. Both have a projector, a whiteboard and fast Wi-Fi. Coffee breaks and lunch can be ordered from the Orbit restaurant.

## Cancellation
You can cancel for free until 48 hours before arrival. After that, we charge the price of the first night. Non-refundable rates cannot be cancelled or changed.

## Lost and found
Items found in rooms are kept at reception for 3 months. We can send them to you by post if you pay the shipping costs.

## Emergency
In an emergency, call reception by pressing 0 on the room phone. The emergency exits are marked with green signs on every floor. The closest pharmacy is 200 metres from the hotel on Garden Street and is open until 22:00.

## Things to do nearby
The Old Bazaar is a 10-minute walk from the hotel. It has more than 300 small shops selling spices, carpets, jewellery and hand-made ceramics. Most shops open at 09:00 and close at 20:00, but many close for lunch between 13:00 and 15:00.
The City Museum is 15 minutes away by tram. It has a large collection of old maps, coins and musical instruments. Entry is free on the first Sunday of every month.
Sunset Hill is a small park with a view of the whole city. It is popular with photographers in the evening. The walk up takes about 25 minutes, and there is a tea house at the top.
The river walk starts behind the hotel and follows the water for 4 km. It is flat and good for running or cycling. Bikes can be rented at reception for €8 per day.
For food, try the night market on Thursday and Friday evenings. It is famous for grilled corn, fresh juice and saffron ice cream.

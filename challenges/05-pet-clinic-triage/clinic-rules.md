# Happy Paws Clinic – Message Routing Rules

Every message gets exactly ONE label.

## EMERGENCY (we call back within 5 minutes)
- The pet ate something toxic: chocolate, grapes, raisins, xylitol (sugar-free gum), antifreeze, rat poison. This is an emergency EVEN IF the pet seems fine.
- Breathing problems, pale or blue gums.
- A seizure, even if it has stopped.
- Hit by a car or any vehicle, EVEN IF the pet can walk.
- Bleeding that does not stop.
- A male cat that tries to pee but nothing comes out.
- A rabbit that has not eaten for 12 hours or more.

## APPOINTMENT
- Check-ups, vaccinations, grooming, nail trims.
- Problems that are not on the emergency list, for example: itching, a mild limp, vomiting ONCE when the pet is now acting normally.

## BILLING
- Only about money that already exists: invoices, receipts, payments, double charges, refunds, insurance forms.
- Questions about the PRICE of a service we have not done yet are OTHER, not BILLING.

## OTHER
- Opening hours, parking, prices, jobs, thank-you messages, lost and found, updating contact details.
- Questions ABOUT our emergency service (for example opening hours) are OTHER when there is no sick pet.

## More than one topic?
Choose the most urgent label: EMERGENCY > APPOINTMENT > BILLING > OTHER.

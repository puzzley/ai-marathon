# Nimbus Games – Employee Handbook

## 1. Working hours
Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Everyone should be available during core hours. Outside core hours you can choose when you work, as long as you work 40 hours per week.

## 2. Remote work
You can work from home up to 2 days per week. Request your remote days in the HR portal "Orbit" before Friday of the previous week. Your manager can ask you to come to the office for important meetings.

## 3. Annual leave
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid leave per year. For leave longer than 3 days, send your request at least 2 weeks before. Up to 5 unused days can move to the next year.

## 4. Sick leave
If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the same day. If you are sick for more than 3 days in a row, you must send a doctor's note to HR.

## 5. Public holidays
The office is closed on national public holidays. The HR team publishes the full list for the new year every December.

## 6. Equipment
Every employee gets a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so they can lock it remotely.

## 7. Expenses
Submit expense claims in Orbit within 30 days, with a photo of the receipt. During business travel, meals are covered up to €40 per day.

## 8. Business travel
Book all trips through the travel desk. Flights shorter than 6 hours are economy class. For longer flights you may book premium economy.

## 9. Learning budget
Each employee has €1,000 per year for courses, books and conferences. Your manager must approve the purchase before you pay.

## 10. Game perks
Employees get free copies of all Nimbus games and early access to beta versions. Please do not share beta builds or screenshots outside the company.

## 11. Office pets
Dogs are welcome in the office on Fridays only, and only on the 3rd floor. Dogs must be vaccinated and friendly. Cats are not allowed because several colleagues have allergies.

## 12. Kitchen
Coffee, tea and fruit are free. Please wash your own mugs. The fridge is emptied every Friday at 17:00, and everything left inside is thrown away.

## 13. Security
Always wear your badge in the office. Two-factor login is required for all company accounts. Never share your password, not even with IT.

## 14. Parental leave
The main caregiver gets 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The second caregiver gets 4 weeks of paid leave.

## 15. Performance reviews
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in March and September. You and your manager prepare together and agree on goals for the next six months.

## 16. Code of conduct
Treat everyone with respect. We do not accept harassment or discrimination of any kind. Report problems to HR or to any manager you trust.

## 17. Overtime
We do not have mandatory overtime. Overtime of more than 4 hours in one week needs approval from a director. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times your normal hourly rate.

## 18. Referral bonus
If you recommend someone and we hire them, you receive €2,000 after they pass their 3-month probation period.

## 19. Leaving the company
The notice period is 1 month. Return all equipment on your last day.

# New Team Laptops – Requirements

Every laptop must meet **all** of these:
1. Memory (RAM): at least 16 GB
2. Storage (SSD): at least 512 GB
3. Weight: at most 1.5 kg
4. Battery life: at least 10 hours
5. Price: at most €1,500
6. Linux: officially supported by the maker

Nice to have (only to break a tie):
- At least 2 USB-C ports
- Backlit keyboard

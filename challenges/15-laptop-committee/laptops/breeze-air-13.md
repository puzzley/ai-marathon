# Breeze Air 13 – Product Sheet

Light, quiet and beautiful. The Breeze Air 13 has no fan, so it is completely silent, even under load. The 13.3-inch screen has great colours and a thin border.

**Performance.** The efficient 8-core chip comes with 8 GB of unified memory, which is perfect for email, documents and web apps. Storage: 512 GB SSD.

**Portability.** The Breeze Air weighs only 1.1 kg and is 11 mm thin. With up to 14 hours of battery life, it is our best laptop for travel.

**Software.** Comes with Windows 11. Linux is officially supported, and we publish drivers for Ubuntu and Fedora.

**Ports and extras.** 2 × USB-C and a headphone jack. Backlit keyboard.

**Price.** €1,099 including VAT. 2-year warranty.

Memory cannot be upgraded after purchase.

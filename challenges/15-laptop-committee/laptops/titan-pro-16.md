# Titan Pro 16 – Product Sheet

When you need raw power, the Titan Pro 16 delivers. It is built for machine learning, video editing and huge builds.

**Performance.** 16-core processor, 32 GB of memory and a 1 TB SSD. A dedicated graphics card with 8 GB of video memory handles heavy workloads.

**Screen.** A 16-inch 165 Hz screen with 100% colour coverage.

**Portability.** The Titan Pro weighs 2.1 kg. Battery life is up to 8 hours of light use. The 180 W charger is included.

**Software.** Windows 11 Pro. Linux is officially supported, with drivers for the latest Ubuntu LTS.

**Ports and extras.** 3 × USB-C, 2 × USB-A, HDMI, SD card reader, Ethernet. RGB backlit keyboard.

**Price.** €1,899 including VAT. 3-year warranty with on-site service.

# Nova X14 – Product Sheet

The Nova X14 is our favourite laptop for developers who want power without the weight. Many developers choose it for its excellent keyboard and bright screen.

**Performance.** 12-core processor, 16 GB of memory (8 GB soldered + 8 GB in a free slot, upgradeable to 40 GB) and a 1 TB SSD.

**Portability.** 1.4 kg, with up to 11 hours of battery life for office work.

**Software.** Ships with Windows 11 Home. Linux is not officially supported: we do not test or publish drivers for Linux, and warranty support covers Windows only.

**Ports and extras.** 2 × USB-C, 1 × USB-A, HDMI. Backlit keyboard.

**Price.** €1,449 including VAT. 2-year warranty.

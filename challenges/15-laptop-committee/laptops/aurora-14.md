# Aurora 14 – Product Sheet

Meet the Aurora 14, the laptop for people who build things. Its aluminium body is milled from one block, so it feels solid in your bag and on your desk. The 14-inch 2.8K screen shows sharp text for long coding sessions, and the anti-glare coating helps in bright offices.

**Performance.** An 8-core processor with 16 GB of memory runs your IDE, your browser and a few containers at the same time. The fast 512 GB SSD opens large projects in seconds.

**Portability.** At just 1,350 g, the Aurora 14 is easy to carry from meeting room to meeting room. The battery gives you up to 12 hours of mixed use, so you can leave the charger at home.

**Software.** The Aurora 14 is certified for Ubuntu Linux and also ships with Windows 11. Our support team helps with both systems.

**Ports and extras.** 2 × USB-C (Thunderbolt), 1 × USB-A, HDMI and a headphone jack. The keyboard is backlit with three brightness levels. The fingerprint reader is in the power button.

**Price.** €1,399 including VAT. 3-year warranty.

Designers, writers and engineers love the Aurora. Reviewers call it "the laptop you forget you are carrying".

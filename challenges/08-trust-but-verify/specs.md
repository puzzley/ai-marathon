# Nimbus Kitchen – Kettle Spec Sheet

## Nimbus Kettle K1
- Capacity: 1.5 litres.
- Power: 2,200 watts.
- Body material: stainless steel.
- Temperature control: none. The K1 only boils water.
- Automatic shut-off: yes, when the water boils or when the kettle is empty.
- Weight: 1.1 kg.
- Colours: white and black.
- Warranty: 2 years.
- Price: €39.

## Nimbus Kettle K2 Pro
- Capacity: 1.7 litres.
- Power: 2,400 watts.
- Body material: glass with a stainless steel base.
- Temperature presets: 70 °C, 80 °C, 90 °C and 100 °C.
- Keep-warm function: yes, for up to 30 minutes.
- Connectivity: none. The K2 Pro has no Wi-Fi and no app.
- Automatic shut-off: yes.
- Weight: 1.4 kg.
- Colours: silver.
- Warranty: 3 years.
- Price: €79.

## Nimbus Travel Kettle
- Capacity: 0.5 litres.
- Power: 1,000 watts.
- Body material: plastic (BPA-free) with a foldable silicone body.
- Temperature control: none. The Travel Kettle only boils water.
- Dual voltage: 110–240 V, for use in most countries.
- Automatic shut-off: yes.
- Weight: 0.6 kg.
- Colours: grey.
- Warranty: 1 year.
- Price: €29.

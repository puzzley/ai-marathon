# ShopRight – Customer Policies

## 1. Ordering
You can order on our website or in our mobile app. An order is confirmed when you receive the confirmation email. If you do not receive it within one hour, please check your spam folder or contact us. We may cancel an order if an item is out of stock; in that case you get a full refund within 5 working days.

## 2. Payment methods
We accept credit cards, debit cards and ShopRight gift cards. You can split one payment between one gift card and one card. We do not accept cash on delivery. Prices include VAT.

## 3. Shipping times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 working days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 working days and costs €7.90. The promised delivery date is shown in your order confirmation. International orders can take longer because of customs checks.

## 4. Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free for orders over €50. For smaller orders it costs €4.90. Express shipping always costs €7.90.

## 5. Change of address
The delivery address of an order can only be changed before the order ships. After it ships, we must ask the courier to change it. Updating the address in your customer profile does NOT change the address of orders that were already placed. Please contact support so we can update the order with the courier.

## 6. Late deliveries
If an order arrives more than 3 days after the promised delivery date, the customer receives a €10 voucher for the next order. The voucher is sent by email after the order is delivered. Delays caused by customs checks also count as late deliveries.

## 7. Failed deliveries
If the courier cannot deliver a parcel, it goes back to the nearest hub. The courier tries again up to two times. After two failed attempts, the parcel returns to our warehouse and the order is refunded.

## 8. Returns
You can return unused items within 30 days of delivery. Start a return in your account under "My orders". Return shipping is free for customers in the EU. The refund is paid to the original payment method within 5 working days after we receive the item.

## 9. Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days. We send a replacement or refund the item, whichever you prefer. You do not need to return the damaged item.

## 10. Warranty
All electronic products have a 2-year warranty. The warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents, water or wrong use. To use the warranty, contact support with your order number and a description of the problem.

## 11. Gift cards
Gift cards are valid for 3 years. They cannot be exchanged for cash. A lost gift card can be blocked if you have the gift card number.

## 12. Gift wrapping
Gift wrapping costs €3 per item and must be chosen before the order ships.

## 13. Price changes
If the price of an item drops within 7 days after your order, we refund the difference. Contact support with your order number.

## 14. Newsletter
You can subscribe to our newsletter to receive offers. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email.

## 15. Customer accounts
Keep your password secret. We will never ask for your password by email or phone. You can delete your account at any time in the account settings. Open orders must be finished before an account can be deleted.

## 16. Privacy
We only use your personal data to process your orders and, if you agree, to send you our newsletter. We do not sell personal data. You can ask for a copy of your data at any time.

## 17. Reviews
You can write a review for any product you bought. Reviews are checked before they are published. We do not publish reviews with personal data, links or offensive language.

## 18. Support hours
Our support team answers chat messages from 08:00 to 20:00, Monday to Saturday. Emails are answered within 24 hours on working days.

# ShopRight – Product FAQ

## Coffee Grinder Pro
**How many grind settings does it have?** 30 settings, from very fine (espresso) to coarse (French press).
**How much coffee does it hold?** The bean container holds 250 g.
**How do I clean it?** Remove the upper burr and use the brush that comes in the box. Never wash the burrs with water.
**Is it loud?** It is about as loud as a hair dryer, for a few seconds.
**Does it have a timer?** Yes, you can set the grinding time from 1 to 60 seconds.

## Kettle
**What is the capacity?** 1.7 litres.
**Which colours are available?** White and black.
**Does it switch off automatically?** Yes, when the water boils or when there is no water inside.
**How do I remove limescale?** Use descaling tablets once a month if your water is hard.
**Is the kettle cordless?** Yes, it sits on a 360° base.

## Wireless Mouse
**What battery does it use?** One AA battery, included.
**How long does the battery last?** About 12 months with normal use.
**Does it work with Mac and Linux?** Yes, it works with Windows, macOS and Linux without drivers.
**Is it silent?** Yes, the clicks are almost silent.

## Desk Lamp
**Can I change the light colour?** Yes, from warm white to cool white in 5 steps.
**Does it have a USB port?** Yes, a USB-C port to charge your phone.
**What is the power use?** 8 watts at full brightness.

## Backpack
**How big is it?** 25 litres, with a padded pocket for laptops up to 15.6 inches.
**Is it waterproof?** It is water-resistant, not waterproof. It protects against light rain.
**Does it have a warranty?** Yes, 2 years against manufacturing defects.

## Bluetooth Speaker
**How long does the battery last?** Up to 15 hours at medium volume.
**Is it waterproof?** Yes, it can be under 1 metre of water for 30 minutes.
**Can I connect two speakers?** Yes, you can pair two speakers for stereo sound.

## Yoga Mat
**How thick is it?** 6 mm.
**What is it made of?** Natural rubber with a non-slip top layer.
**How do I clean it?** Wipe it with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not put it in the washing machine.

## Water Bottle
**How long does it keep drinks cold?** Up to 24 hours cold and 12 hours hot.
**Is it dishwasher safe?** The lid is dishwasher safe; wash the bottle by hand.
**What sizes are available?** 500 ml and 750 ml.

## Notebook Set
**What is inside?** Three A5 notebooks: lined, dotted and blank, 96 pages each.
**Is the paper recycled?** Yes, 100% recycled paper.

## Phone Case
**Which phones does it fit?** See the list of models on the product page.
**Does it support wireless charging?** Yes, you do not need to remove the case.

# Taskly – Help Center FAQ

## F1. How do I reset my password?
Go to Settings > Security > Reset password. We email you a link. The link works for 30 minutes. If it expired, request a new one.

## F2. What plans do you have?
Free (up to 3 projects), Pro (€8 per user per month), Team (€15 per user per month).

## F3. How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Billing > Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

## F4. Can I get a refund?
Yes, we give a full refund within 14 days of your first payment. After 14 days we do not refund.

## F5. How do I export my data?
Open a project, then Project menu > Export. You can choose CSV or JSON.

## F6. How do I invite teammates?
Go to the Team page > Invite. The Free plan allows a maximum of 2 members. Pro and Team have no limit.

## F7. Does Taskly work offline?
Offline mode is available in the mobile app only. Your changes sync when you are online again. The web and desktop versions need an internet connection.

## F8. Which integrations do you support?
Slack, Google Calendar and GitHub.

## F9. How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete. After 7 days the deletion is permanent.

## F10. How do I turn on two-factor login?
Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor login and scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
